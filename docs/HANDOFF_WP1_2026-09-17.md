## Decisions fixed before model observation

- `wp1-support-v1` specifies the common envelope and selected-file freeze hashes. The scope is binary premise/claim support; `not_supported` is not equivalent to contradiction.
- Inputs are nonempty strings, at most 4,096 characters each; models abstain above 512 formatted input tokens without truncation. Candidate adapters receive no fixture label/category. One invocation, no retries, retrieval, tools or training.
- Exact-match baseline: trimmed casefold equality gives support, else abstention; score unavailable. Learned support/entailment score strictly above/below 0.5 maps to support/non-support, equality abstains. No calibration claim.
- HHEM revision `8e4a2e6e96c708cc76c2344f7e4757df2515292c`; foundation tokenizer/config `7bcac572ce56db69c1ea7c8af255c5d7c9672fc2`; NLI `9a77395d4d3751be9e2a69c4ae318491d9b3fffb`. - Twelve visible author-created synthetic fixtures, six support and six non-support references, no held-out split.
